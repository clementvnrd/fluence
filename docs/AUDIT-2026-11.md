### A3-4 — PR69: the mic spool is uploaded before echo cancellation; on speakers the echo backstop drops the user's reply or keeps the remote's words as « Vous »

### A6-1 — A non-UTF-8 dictionary.txt loads as empty and the next in-app edit overwrites it with no backup
*bug · confidence high · Sources/Fluence/Dictionary/DictionaryStore.swift:48-60, Sources/Fluence/Dictionary/DictionaryStore.swift:156-164, Sources/Fluence/Dictionary/DictionaryStore.swift:191-198, Sources/Fluence/Dictionary/DictionaryStore.swift:211-215*

- **Scenario:** The user edits dictionary.txt by hand (the app invites it) and saves it as Latin-1, Mac Roman or UTF-16, or pastes it from a Windows tool.
